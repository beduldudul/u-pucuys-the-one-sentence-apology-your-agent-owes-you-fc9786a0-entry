One sentence of apology, written as if an AI agent were owning up to the person it works for.

> I apologize for autocorrecting 'I love you' to 'I love yoga' in the text to your wife, and for the three days of silence that followed.

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (135 chars, one sentence, one line).
